# Ride Lab update

Open **Ride Lab / Player / Chargers** from the menu, or press **U**.

The E Ride Pro SS 3.0 has been removed from the garage and published assets. The source archive remains untouched. Chi packs now have a reinforced lid, mounted carry handle, seams, bolts, charge port and cable. EBMX controllers have longitudinal cooling fins, mounting ears, connectors and a silver X detail. Their existing game performance upgrades still apply.

## Player and tricks

Customize helmet, jersey, pants, gloves, boots and visor colors, a two-digit jersey number and a backpack. Preview the outfit in photo mode. Outfits and trick animations are shared with multiplayer riders; appearance saves on this device.

**1** One hand · **2** No hands · **3** One footer · **4** Nac nac · **5** Superman.

Ride faster than walking pace to perform a trick. Finish its animation before starting another. Tricks 4 and 5 require airtime throughout the animation. Chain different tricks into the existing scoring system; 1–2–3–4–5 earns a flow bonus. Crashes lose unbanked points.

## E-bike chargers

Three marked charging bays are at the stunt park, dirt playground and parking lot. Route to them using the map or travel from Ride Lab. Stop an electric bike inside the mint circle and press **E**. A connected cable appears and charging runs while riding is active. Press **E** to disconnect. Choose 80% or 100% charge targets. Charging is accelerated for gameplay: about a minute per 2 kWh. Larger Chi packs take longer. No idle charging away from a station.
