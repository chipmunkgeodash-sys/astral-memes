# Another PSX Style Low-poly Bike

Author: **Duhgless**

Source: https://duhgless.itch.io/another-psx-style-low-poly-bike

Downloaded 2026-09-18. The author's "More information" section lists
**Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal** and "No generative AI was used."

License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Legal text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

Included original files: `Bike3.blend`, `bike3.glb`, and the four `B-*.png`
textures. The GLB embeds its textures and identifies its exporter as
Khronos glTF Blender I/O v3.6.28. The Blender source and separate textures
are retained together for editing; relink textures locally if Blender
reports the author's original texture paths as missing.

The game applies metre scaling, orientation, wheel and steering pivots at
runtime. It labels this original design **Custom Bobber**, with estimated
handling. This asset is not an exact model of any of the seven branded bikes.
