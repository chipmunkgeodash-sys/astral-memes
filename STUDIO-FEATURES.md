## Hush Moto gas stations

West plaza and East plaza now have orange fuel bays, pumps and canopies. Choose a gas bike, stop inside the bay, and press E to refuel. Press E again to stop. Fuel burns while riding; empty tanks stop engine power. Electric bikes continue to use mint charging bays. Find stations in Ride Lab or on the map.
